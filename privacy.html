Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Who we are

Unlimit Music is a free web app that helps songwriters explore chord and melody music theory. This policy explains what data we handle and the cookies we use.

Cookies & advertising

We use cookies to show relevant ads and to measure site traffic. We use Google AdSense to serve advertising. AdSense may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland, consent is collected through Google's certified consent management platform. By default no advertising cookies are set until you grant consent in that message. In other regions ads may load with your continued use of the site, and you can manage or withdraw your choices at any time via Google Ads Settings or the consent controls shown on your first visit.

Data we collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information. Standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by our hosting provider to deliver the site.

Your rights (UK / GDPR)

Under UK GDPR and PECR you may withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's local storage in your browser. For any request, contact us via the site domain owner.
