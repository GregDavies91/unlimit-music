*Unlimit Music is a free web app that puts chord and melody music theory at the fingertips of songwriters — tap a root note to hear it, then tap any chord to see the diagram and hear how it's played.*

## Authorship

Unlimit Music was created by **Ian "Ron" Davies**, who first built it as a cardboard chord/scale teaching tool, then as a guitar chord app on the Apple App Store. It is continued here as a free web app by his son, **Greg Davies**, including the chord reference and the chromatic "scales wheel" melody instrument based on Ron's original design. All underlying creative work originates with Ron.
