Free Guitar Chords & Scales for Songwriters

Unlimit Music is a free web app that helps you explore chord and melody music theory. Tap a root note on the left to hear it, tap any chord to see the guitar diagram and hear how it's played. Use the chromatic scales wheel to find melodies that fit your progression.

How to use it

  1. Pick a key — tap a root note (C through B) on the rainbow column on the left.
  2. Browse chords — the grid fills with every chord in that key. Tap any chord to hear it and see the diagram.
  3. Build a progression — try I–V–vi–IV (e.g. C–G–Am–F) and listen to how they fit together.
  4. Find a melody — use the scales wheel on the right. Drag the ring to rotate through keys. Tap any orb to hear the note.
  5. Explore scales — tap "Scale" in the top bar to switch between major, minor, pentatonic, blues, modes, and more.
